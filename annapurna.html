About the Annapurna Trek

The Annapurna Circuit is famed for its incredible variety of landscapes — from subtropical lowlands and terraced farmlands to high alpine deserts and snow-capped peaks. Trekkers traverse lush forests, cascading waterfalls, glacial streams, and stark high-altitude plateaus.

Cultural immersion is a highlight. Visit Gurung and Thakali villages, observe traditional farming, explore ancient temples and monasteries, and witness local festivals. The trail offers rich insights into Himalayan heritage and lifestyle.

Crossing Thorong La Pass (5,416 m) provides panoramic views of Annapurna, Dhaulagiri, Machhapuchhre, and Nilgiri ranges. This 14–18 day trek is moderate to strenuous but immensely rewarding.

Trekkers encounter alpine meadows dotted with wildflowers, Himalayan wildlife like snow leopards and blue sheep, and breathtaking sunrises over the mountains. Spring and Autumn are the best seasons for clear skies and vibrant scenery.

The Annapurna Circuit Trek is a complete Himalayan adventure combining physical challenge, cultural immersion, and stunning natural beauty.
