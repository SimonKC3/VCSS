Mystical Journey to Dolpo’s Turquoise Lake

About Shey Phoksundo Lake Trek

Shey Phoksundo Lake Trek in Dolpo region is one of Nepal’s most remote and mystical trekking adventures. The trek takes you to the turquoise waters of Phoksundo Lake, the deepest lake in Nepal, surrounded by towering cliffs and snow-capped peaks.

Along the route, trekkers encounter traditional Tibetan Buddhist culture, ancient monasteries, and nomadic communities. The Dolpo region is renowned for its pristine wilderness, wildlife, and tranquil landscapes.

Trekkers traverse high-altitude valleys, past waterfalls, rugged trails, and meadows, making it a physically challenging yet spiritually enriching journey. Best seasons are Spring (March–May) and Autumn (September–November) for optimal weather and clear skies.

Shey Phoksundo Trek is perfect for those seeking adventure, solitude, and a deep connection with Nepal’s natural and cultural heritage.
